Positions

What I think, and what would change it

As of September 2026

An eval you cannot re-run next quarter is not an eval.

Golden sets drift, annotators disagree with their own past selves, and a benchmark living in a notebook dies with the kernel. Version it with the model, run it on a schedule, leave an artifact a reviewer can open.

What would change my mind: a judge model holding agreement with expert reviewers across a year of drift, without recalibration.

Most agent loops are retry loops with better branding.

A loop that calls tools until satisfied can spend unbounded money and take unbounded actions. The engineering is the stopping condition, the budget and the replay trail — not the planning.

What would change my mind: frameworks treating the transcript as a replayable artifact the way MLflow treats a training run.

Retrieval is a ranking problem wearing a generation costume.

If the right passage is not in the top-k, no prompt work saves the answer — it just makes the wrong one fluent. Measure recall@k first. Keep lexical search: exact codes are where embeddings are weakest.

What would change my mind: long context making retrieval quality irrelevant at enterprise corpus sizes. The cost curve says otherwise.
